# Blame Fighter

A 44-second vertical satire reel (1080×1920, 30 fps) in the style of a retro
fighting game. The finished file is
[`blame-fighter-reel.mp4`](blame-fighter-reel.mp4). It is signed "anonymous".

MNF, Congress and ZPM appear as three selectable fighters with identical
stats. The joke is the accusation cycle: parties demand corruption probes in
opposition and blame the previous government once in power. The reel does not
say any party or person committed corruption, and it treats all three the same.

What it draws on, from news reports in 2026:

- MNF and Congress both demanded a CBI probe into a land deal under the ZPM
  government.
- The ZPM government blamed the previous MNF administration for the same deal.
- Consent for a CBI probe was granted, and the opposition says nothing followed.
- Congress accused the ZPM government of failing its clean-governance promises.

Every number in the reel (the scoreboard, the stat bars) is a joke, not data.
The artwork, text, music and sound effects are original and generated by the
files here; nothing is taken from an existing game.

## Rebuilding it

```bash
python3 audio.py
node render.mjs
```
