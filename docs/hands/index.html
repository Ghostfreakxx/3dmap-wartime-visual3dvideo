Hand Synth

Play music with your hands

Your hands become the instrument. Raise and lower one hand to change the note, pinch to open the filter, spread both hands to make it liquid, and close a fist for a drum hit. Sparks and spell circles follow your fingers.

OSC FREQ
Height of your left hand (or first finger)
FILTER
Pinch thumb and index on your right hand
LIQUID
Distance between your two hands
DRUM
Close a fist (or double-tap)

The camera needs a browser page you open directly, such as Safari or Chrome on your phone. Touch mode works everywhere.
