Hand Synth

Play music with your hands

Your hands become the instrument. Raise and lower one hand to change the note, pinch to open the filter, bring your hands together to charge an energy orb, and clap for a shockwave.

OSC FREQ
Height of your left hand (or first finger)
FILTER
Pinch thumb and index on your right hand
LIQUID
Distance between your two hands
DRUMS
Fist = kick · quick pinch = hi-hat · clap = snare and cymbal
MAGIC
Spell circles, sparks, glitch blocks and lightning between your hands. Flick a hand upward for a spiral of equations
