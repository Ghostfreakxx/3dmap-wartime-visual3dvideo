Hand Synth

Your body is an instrument

Five ways to play light and sound with your hands. Switch between them at the top of the screen.

INTERFACE
A planet sits in your palm inside rotating gauges. Turn your hand to turn them; open it to fill them. Height sets the note.
CRADLE
Strings run between your fingertips. Stretch or squeeze them to pluck a harp.
SIGIL
A constellation circle blooms in your palm. Hold it open to charge, and it fires a beam of light.
GRAVITY
Your index finger bends space and the grid like a black hole. Pinch for more force; a peace sign pushes instead of pulls.
CLASSIC
The full synth with sparks, lightning, air drawing and equation spirals.
