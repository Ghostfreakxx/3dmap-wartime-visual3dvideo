## D. Interpretation and wording

- **D1. "Cession" vs "renunciation" at Yandabo.** Narration uses "renounced all claims", from Article 2.
- **D2. "Tributary".** Ahom tributary status after 1663 was nominal and short-lived. Kachari and Jaintia submission in 1708 was likewise temporary. Do not colour these areas as Ahom territory.
- **D3. Causes of the Moamoria rebellion.** Present several explanations.
- **D4. Heroic traditions around Lachit.** Label them tradition, and note the role of Bhuyan's 1947 work in building the modern narrative.
- **D5. Neighbouring peoples.** Avoid the language of "savage hill tribes" found in colonial sources. Name the communities, and describe relationships such as raids, trade and posa arrangements neutrally.
- **D6. Modern identity politics** (Saikia 2004). Avoid triumphalist framing that maps onto present-day disputes.
